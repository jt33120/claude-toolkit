# Content allocation examples

Use these as decision examples, not fixed layouts or mandatory text budgets.

## Cost comparison

Brief: populations, reference costs, proposed services, a savings estimate, formulas, assumptions, technical architecture and rollout constraints.

- **Visible:** total reference/projected cost and period, estimate status, comparable horizontal bars by population, the next useful action.
- **Exploration:** select a population or savings bar to inspect the breakdown.
- **Detail:** show the exact calculation, inputs and relevant assumptions from that selection.
- **Spec:** full architecture, sources, rollout constraints, rejected alternatives and derivations.

Do not put a title, subtitle, explanation paragraph and duplicate KPI card around each bar. Keep an assumption visible if changing it could reverse the displayed conclusion. Do not invent savings or hide costs to produce a cleaner chart.

## Settings

Brief: common preferences, advanced routing parameters, explanations of backend services, validation rules.

- **Visible:** common settings, current values, clear labels and Save.
- **Exploration:** an Advanced settings disclosure for uncommon controls.
- **Detail:** constraints and recovery near the advanced control when needed.
- **Spec:** service architecture and reasons behind defaults.

Keep required format guidance and errors near their fields. Do not move every explanation into tooltips, and do not replace familiar settings with a diagram.

## Daily operations

Brief: a dense queue with statuses, owners, deadlines, exact amounts and prioritization rules.

- **Visible:** the sortable table and filters needed to handle the queue; density serves frequent scanning.
- **Exploration:** selecting a row opens its work context.
- **Detail:** history and evidence remain available from that row.
- **Spec:** prioritization rules, field semantics, integration assumptions.

Do not replace the useful table with decorative charts or force each row through a separate wizard. Keep key comparisons visible together.
