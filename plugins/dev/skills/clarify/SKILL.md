# Clarify

Read [frontend-content](../frontend-content/SKILL.md), the surrounding flow and existing terminology. Preserve factual meaning and the product's voice.

- Name actions by their outcome with a concrete verb; keep the same action's name throughout the flow.
- Say each idea once. Drop helper text that repeats a label; keep guidance that prevents an error.
- Use persistent field labels. Treat placeholders as examples and retain required format guidance.
- State what failed and how to recover. Distinguish empty data, filtered-out results, permissions and failures; do not invent causes or progress.
- Keep price, eligibility, consent and destructive consequences explicit at the decision point.
- Replace internal jargon with user vocabulary while retaining domain terms the audience needs.
- Put uncommon explanations behind descriptive, accessible controls; never rely on hover alone or icon-only meaning.

Update final strings and any moved context in the companion spec. Check terminology, long values, translation expansion, keyboard/screen-reader labels and narrow layouts. Keep wording as short as possible while preserving meaning and recovery.
