# Sales battlecard structure

One page, for sales/marketing use against a specific competitor.

- **Header** — competitor name, last-updated date, win rate if tracked.
- **Quick overview** — what they do (one sentence), target customer, pricing model, recent developments.
- **Their pitch** — self-description, tagline, top 3 claimed differentiators.
- **Strengths (honest)** — where they genuinely compete well, what customers like (from reviews).
- **Weaknesses** — consistent complaints, technical limits, gaps.
- **Our differentiators** — 3-5, each with why it matters to the customer and proof.
- **Objection handling table**:

| If the prospect says... | Respond with... |
|---|---|
| "[Competitor] does X too" | How the approach differs |
| "[Competitor] is cheaper" | What the price difference buys |
| "I've heard good things about [Competitor]" | Where they're strong; where we differ |

- **Landmines to set** — early discovery questions that surface your advantage.
- **Win/loss themes** — common reasons deals are won and lost against this competitor.

Review quarterly at minimum, and immediately after a major competitor announcement.
