# Watch-based post (security news for a professional audience)

Use when the content comes from a watch report: one news, one action, one question.

## Post
- First line: the fact, with its number, readable before "see more".
- Then what changes, the impact, and the team's reading ("Our reading: …") in one sentence.
- A concrete question for the audience.
- Sources named in the post; links in the first comment.
- No promotion of the author's own services. No unverified figure. A lab demonstration is never presented as an observed attack.
- Short: about 500–850 characters. Lists with "→".

## Carousel (actions first)
1. Page 1: the fact as a hook (big number) + "What to do in your organisation" (3 actions, 4 at most).
2. Middle pages: the proof — what happened, the attack chain, key figures, what held. Source and reliability on every page.
3. Last page: the team's reading + the question.
Every page is filled by a visual (real logo, diagram, pictograms, key figure); no page of text and empty space. Deliver numbered PNG images (1080×1350), never a PDF.

## Video
Hook with the fact (readable without sound in 2 seconds), the actions right after the brand, then the proof, then the question. Motion design by default; free-licence stock footage only for context, never AI-generated footage, credits recorded.

## Newsletter
Title, 2–3 sentence summary, the "key takeaways" box right under it, then one card per news (type · reliability, title, 2–3 sentences ending with a "To do", source links).
