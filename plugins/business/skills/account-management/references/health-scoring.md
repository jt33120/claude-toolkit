# Account management — scoring tables and templates

## Customer health dimensions

| Dimension | Signal | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Relationship | champion/exec engagement, breadth of active contacts | green/yellow/red |
| Engagement trend | meeting + email volume vs. prior period | |
| Commercial | renewal proximity, open expansion conversation, payment/contract issues | |
| Support | open escalations, aging tickets (if visible) | |
| Value delivery | documented outcomes vs. the success plan, if one exists | |

Overall verdict names the one or two dimensions actually driving it — never average silently into a single number with no explanation.

## Renewal risk signals

| Signal | Effect |
|---|---|
| No activity on the account in 30+ days | risk up |
| Champion or economic buyer changed/left | risk up |
| Open unresolved escalation | risk up |
| Usage/adoption trending down (if tracked) | risk up |
| Active expansion conversation in flight | risk down / uplift potential up |
| Multi-year or auto-renew terms | risk down |

Verdict per renewal: on track / needs attention / at risk, with the evidence named.

## QBR agenda structure

1. Value delivered since last review — in the customer's own metrics, sourced
2. Adoption story — what's working, what's underused (visible facts only)
3. Open items — escalations resolved/open, prior commitments
4. Next phase — expansion plays worth raising, renewal framing
5. Attendees and asks — who should be in the room per the stakeholder map, what to ask their execs for

## Expansion whitespace grid dimensions

Products/tiers · Teams/departments · Geography/subsidiaries · Volume/usage headroom — each row: owned today, the whitespace, and the evidence for it. Discard anything with no evidence into a "parking lot" with the signal that would promote it later.

## Stakeholder map roles

Champion (must have taken concrete action for you) · Economic buyer · Evaluator · Influencer · Blocker · Unknown. Engagement: active (recently met) / warm / cold / never met. Stance: positive / neutral / negative / unknown — mark unknown honestly rather than guessing.

## Account plan sections

1. Account snapshot — what they do, size, current relationship
2. Their goals and initiatives — cited from calls/email/public sources
3. Where we are — open/closed deals, wins/losses and why
4. Stakeholder coverage — who's known, who's missing
5. Opportunity map — expansion hypotheses with evidence
6. Risks — competitive presence, renewal exposure, champion risk
7. Action plan — dated actions with owners for the planning horizon
