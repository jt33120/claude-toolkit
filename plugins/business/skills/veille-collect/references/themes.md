# Watch themes and search patterns

Run each theme in French and English. Combine the theme terms with the AI terms (LLM, AI agent, agentic, model, prompt injection, MCP, RAG, deepfake, AI-generated) unless the theme is already AI-specific.

## Attacks and incidents
- Observed attacks using AI (phishing, fraud, deepfake, offensive agents, AI-written malware).
- Attacks *against* AI systems (prompt injection in production, agent hijacking, model theft, data poisoning).
- Primary sources: CERTs, incident reports from the victim or the investigating vendor, court or police statements.

## Vulnerabilities
- CVEs in AI frameworks, inference servers, agent platforms, MCP servers, coding assistants, AI gateways.
- Primary sources: CVE record, vendor advisory, researcher write-up, exploited-vulnerability catalogues.

## Regulation
- EU: AI Act and its guidelines, delegated acts and harmonised standards; NIS2; DORA; Cyber Resilience Act; GDPR guidance on AI.
- France: transposition laws and decrees, national cyber agency and data protection authority guidance.
- Standards: ISO/IEC 42001 and related, CEN-CENELEC work on AI.
- Primary sources only: EUR-Lex, official journals, the Commission, national authorities, standard bodies.

## Competition
- Consulting firms selling AI security or AI governance; AI security vendors (model, agent, data, runtime); red-teaming labs.
- Signals: new offer or feature, pricing page change, funding, acquisition, partnership, published research, hiring for AI security roles.
- Primary sources: the actor's site, press release, changelog, repository, official registry.

## Research
- Papers and lab publications on attacks, defences, evaluations and containment of AI systems.
- Primary sources: the paper, the lab's post, the dataset or code release.

## Buying signals
- Public tenders mentioning AI security, AI governance or AI Act compliance; organisations announcing an AI incident; security leadership hires.
- Primary sources: tender platforms, the organisation's own announcement.
