# Multi-touch sequence design

## Architecture, before drafting individual emails
- Narrative arc — the story across all touches, from first email to last.
- Journey mapping — each email to a stage (awareness, consideration, decision, activation).
- Escalation logic — how urgency/value builds touch over touch.
- Success definition — the specific action that ends the sequence.

## Per email
Subject (2-3 options: curiosity, benefit, urgency, question-based; under 50 characters), preview text
(40-90 characters, complements not repeats the subject), one-sentence purpose, full body copy (hook → body →
CTA, short paragraphs), one primary CTA, timing (days after trigger or previous email; adjust for
engagement — "send sooner if opened but not clicked"), who receives it vs who skips it.

## Flow control
- **Branching** — e.g. "if opened but didn't click, send a softer re-ask instead of the next touch."
- **Exit conditions** — define conversion for this sequence; remove on conversion.
- **Re-entry** — under what conditions someone re-enters (e.g. a win-back 90 days after churning again).
- **Suppression** — never send if already in another active sequence, unsubscribed, or contacted support
  recently.

## Templates by type (adapt length/content to goal and audience)
| Type | Emails | Span | Arc |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | 5-7 | 14-21 days | Welcome → quick win → core feature → advanced feature → social proof → check-in → upgrade prompt |
| Lead nurture | 4-6 | 3-4 weeks | Value-first content → pain point → solution + proof → social proof → soft CTA → direct CTA |
| Re-engagement | 3-4 | 10-14 days | "We miss you" → value reminder → incentive → last chance with deadline |
| Win-back | 3-5 | 30 days | Check-in → what's new → offer → feedback ask → goodbye, door open |
| Product launch | 4-6 | 2-3 weeks | Teaser → announcement → feature spotlight → social proof → limited offer → reminder |
| Event follow-up | 3-4 | 7-10 days | Thank-you + recording → resource roundup → related offer → feedback survey |
| Educational drip | 5-8 | 4-6 weeks | Intro → lesson 1 → lesson 2 → lesson 3 → application → roundup → next steps |

## Benchmarks (set targets, adjust for industry)
| Metric | Onboarding | Lead nurture | Re-engagement | Win-back |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open rate | 50-70% | 20-30% | 15-25% | 15-20% |
| Click-through | 10-20% | 3-7% | 2-5% | 2-4% |
| Conversion | 15-30% | 2-5% | 3-8% | 1-3% |
| Unsubscribe | <0.5% | <0.5% | 1-2% | 1-3% |

## Output
Sequence overview table (# / subject / purpose / timing / CTA / condition), full email drafts, a text flow
diagram showing branches and exits, a branching-logic reference list, 2-3 A/B test suggestions (subject line,
CTA text, send time), and the metrics to track with a review cadence (weekly for month one, then monthly).
