# Structural AI tells (not caught by the script)

Inspired by Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" field guide and the humanizer skills ecosystem; original wording.

- **Negative parallelism:** "It's not X, it's Y" / « Ce n'est pas X, c'est Y ». State Y directly.
- **Rule of three by reflex:** three adjectives or three bullet points when one or two are true.
- **Signposting:** announcing what you will say, then saying it, then summarizing it.
- **Hedging stack:** "may potentially help to some extent". Commit or cut.
- **Vague significance:** "plays a key role", "marks a turning point". Say what concretely changes.
- **Promotional puffery:** stunning, vibrant, world-class, incontournable. Use a fact instead.
- **Generic closers:** « En somme », "I hope this helps", « N'hésitez pas ». End on the last useful sentence.
- **Synonym cycling:** renaming the same thing every sentence to avoid repetition. Repeat the right word.
- **Title Case Headings** and bold on every other phrase.
- **Uniform rhythm:** every sentence 15–25 words. Mix short and long.
- **Over-formatting:** headings and bullets in a 150-word message.
- **Fake specificity:** round numbers and invented examples. Use real data or none.
- **Sycophancy / meta:** "Great question", « Voici une proposition… », talking about the text instead of writing it.
